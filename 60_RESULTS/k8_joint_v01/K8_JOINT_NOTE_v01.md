## 2. Method: M*J

**Objects** (for one tracker and one log fold f):
- **Base thresholds.** M*'s thresholds (`k7_fits_<t>_v01.json`, `Mstar_ip`), c(f, h, l0, ip) > 0 on U = D / sd. They exist for the 4 calibrated steps h (0.5, 1, 1.5, 2 s), the 96 L0 strata and the in-path flag ip, which gives 192 thresholds per step. Every one of the 2 × 5 × 8 step blocks is > 0: the minimum is 0.054 on T1 and 0.032 on T3 (test J2).
- **Decision.** One planner query at a logged state: (scene, keyframe k) of the calibration items. These are the `k8_items_v02` enforced (track, check time) pairs at the decision-mode states, with ground truth at k + h.
- **Constraints of a decision.** Its `ref_relied` items: one per enforced pair (the axis the planner relies on at the reference pose), over every track and the 4 calibrated steps. These are the arm-independent PD7 items.
  - SD1_7 keeps the pairs whose gap at the reference pose is ≤ 5 m.
  - PD keeps the in-path pairs.
- **Joint loss of a decision at λ.** ℓ_d(λ) = 1{∃ i ∈ d : U_i > λ c_i} = 1{R_d > λ}, with R_d = max_i U_i / c_i.
- **Scene loss.** L_s(λ) is the share of the scene's decisions with ℓ_d(λ) = 1, counting only decisions with ≥ 1 constraint in the population. This is the estimand of V2: per scene, then the mean over scenes.

**The arm.** At the 4 calibrated steps, c_J(f, h, l0, ip) = λ_f · c_M*(f, h, l0, ip). Steps 2.5–4 s keep M*'s K6 thresholds, because the planner does not enforce them.

**Fitting λ.** For each population P ∈ {PD7, SD1_7, PD}:

λ_P = min{ λ ∈ {1.00, 1.01, …, 100.00} : n_P/(n_P+1) · mean_s L_s(λ) + 1/(n_P+1) ≤ δ = 0.05 }

- n_P is the number of calibration scenes with a decision in P.
- This is the rule of `x1_lib_v03.crc_threshold`, with the scene as the unit, on a grid.
- v02 makes the comparison in exact rational arithmetic (§4).
- λ_f = max_P λ_P.
- If no grid point qualifies, the CRC answer is λ = ∞. The grid maximum would then be deployed and flagged; this never happened.

**Stratification, and why this one.**
- The union in ℓ_d couples tracks and steps from different strata in one decision, so per-stratum inflations cannot each be calibrated against their own share of the joint loss.
- A vector of per-stratum inflations has no total order. CRC needs a nested one-dimensional family; with a vector, the problem becomes multiple testing (Learn-then-Test).
- Allocating risk per constraint (δ/n each, Blackmore's Lemma 1) would need about 279 scenes per stratum (P01 Prop. 6). nuScenes does not have that (D14 v05 §9).
- M*J therefore uses **one scalar per tracker and fold, shared by all 192 strata and the 4 steps**, on top of M*'s per-stratum thresholds:
  - M*'s relative shape across strata is kept: it is calibrated per constraint, with fallback L0 → L3 at n_min = 19.
  - One monotone degree of freedom is added and spent on the joint loss.
- λ ≥ 1, so M*J's margins are never smaller than M*'s, record by record.
- The three populations share the one λ through the max. Each keeps its own guarantee (§3); no union bound across populations is used.

**Two variants.**
- **M*J (primary, as specified).** Calibrates on M*'s own calibration scenes (the 4 folds other than f) and uses M*'s fold-f base.
- **M*Jx (exact split, sensitivity).** With g = (f + 1) mod 5:
  - the base is refitted by M*'s own rule (`k7_fit_v01.split_fit`, the same strata, n_min and fallback) on the 3 folds other than f and g;
  - λ is fitted on fold g only.

  It uses the same 4 calibration folds as M*, split into a shape part and an inflation part.

## 3. Why the CRC guarantee holds (proof sketch)

**Conditions.** Take one population, and a base c that is fixed and independent of the calibration and test scenes.
1. **Exchangeability at the scene level.** The n calibration scenes and the new scene are exchangeable, so the random functions L_1, …, L_{n+1} are exchangeable.
2. **Bounded loss.** L_s(λ) ∈ [0, 1], so B = 1.
3. **Monotone in the scalar.** Because c_i > 0, λ ↦ 1{U_i > λ c_i} is non-increasing and right-continuous. A maximum over constraints and a mean over decisions keep both properties. At λ = ∞, L_s = 0 ≤ δ.

**Proof.**
- Let R̂_{n+1}(λ) be the mean of all n + 1 scene losses, and λ' = min{λ ∈ Λ ∪ {∞} : R̂_{n+1}(λ) ≤ δ}.
- Since L_{n+1} ≤ 1, R̂_{n+1}(λ) ≤ n/(n+1) · R̂_n(λ) + 1/(n+1). So every λ that is admissible for λ̂ is admissible for λ', and λ' ≤ λ̂.
- λ' is a symmetric function of (L_1, …, L_{n+1}). By exchangeability, E[L_{n+1}(λ')] = E[R̂_{n+1}(λ')] ≤ δ.
- By monotonicity, L_{n+1}(λ̂) ≤ L_{n+1}(λ'). Hence **E[L_{n+1}(λ̂)] ≤ δ**.

This is Theorem 1 of Angelopoulos, Bates, Fisch, Lei and Schuster, *Conformal Risk Control* (ICLR 2024), with the scene as the exchangeable unit.

**Three populations.** λ_f = max_P λ_P ≥ λ_P, so L^P_{n+1}(λ_f) ≤ L^P_{n+1}(λ_P) pointwise, and E ≤ δ holds for each population (marginally).

**What is certified.** The expected share of a new scene's decisions in which some relied constraint, over every track and the 4 steps, is exceeded at margin λ c sd **at the reference (logged) pose**.

**What is not certified.**
- **The planner's V2 at the chosen plan.** There, the relied axis and the "satisfied at the arm's margin" filter depend on the arm and the plan, and infeasible decisions back off. That quantity is tested empirically in §6.
- **M*J's exact guarantee.** M*J's base is fitted on its own calibration scenes, so condition 1 holds only conditionally on a base that does not depend on them. M*Jx meets the condition exactly. The size of the departure is measured in §5.
- **Independence of scenes within a log.** Scenes of one log are dependent. Scene-level exchangeability is a working assumption shared with M*, and every interval below uses the log-cluster bootstrap.

## 4. Finding: an exact tie at δ in M*'s T3 fit (ERRATA_K8_v02 E15)

**What happened.** The first full T3 fit (`k8_joint_fit_v01`) stopped on its own guard on Windows and on Linux: "the refit of M* from these items differs from the fits (fold 4, h2s)". The guard requires the refit of M* from the items to equal `k7_fits` exactly.

**Diagnosis** (`70_TESTS/k8_joint_v01/diag/k8_joint_t3_refit_diag_v01.json`):
- The items equal M*'s own `k7_items_t3_v01` in every field, for every scene (1,613,073 items; sha 81d265fc… = the fits' `items_sha256`). The data, the folds and the dev-scene items are not the cause.
- Only 1 of the 3,840 thresholds differs: fold 4, h2s, L0 74 (large | <20 m | long | turning, not in path), its own L0 cell, with 243 items from 108 scenes.
- At U = 0.38665 the CRC bound is **exactly 1/20 = δ** in rational arithmetic.
- In floating point, the mean over scenes evaluates to 0.049999999999999996 (accepted) when summed in sorted-name order, and to 0.05000000000000001 (rejected) in K7 v01's scene-index order.
- M*'s fit therefore took the next value, 0.39570, whose exact bound is 89/2180 = 0.041. No value of U lies between the two.
- M*'s items in K7's order reproduce `k7_fits` exactly; the same items relabelled to sorted-name order reproduce the v01 refit exactly.

**What it means.**
- M*'s fits are consistent with their own items under their own computation, but are order-dependent at exact ties.
- Under exact arithmetic the "≤ δ" rule accepts 0.38665, so K7's value is one step more conservative.
- Validity is unaffected; the margin is slightly larger in one stratum, step and fold.
- T1 has no tie that flips.

**Fix in `k8_joint_fit_v02`** (the guard is unchanged):
- M* is refitted in M*'s own scene order, which makes `--k7-items` required. The refit then reproduces `k7_fits` exactly on all 5 folds for both trackers.
- The joint λ decision is made in exact rational arithmetic, so it does not depend on scene order. Float and exact decisions agree in all 60 population fits.
- The T1 v02 fit equals the earlier T1 v01 fit (d7d611a4…) in every threshold.

## 7. Verdict: Form B applied to the joint loss (D14 v05 §6; D16 Choice 2 (b))

**Joint validity rule.** For each of PD7, SD1_7 and PD:
- the scene-level V2 is evaluable (≥ 39 scenes from ≥ 8 logs);
- it is not flagged, meaning its one-sided 0.95 log-bootstrap lower bound does not exceed δ;
- its one-sided 0.95 upper bound is ≤ δ + 0.03.

The same rule is applied to every arm. HK1a's per-stratum family has no analogue, because a decision's union spans strata.

**T1: Form B on the joint loss is MET.**
- M*J is valid: V2 0.048, lower bound 0.0385, upper bound 0.058; SD1_7 0.007; PD 0.002.
- The only comparator valid on the joint loss is B3 (0.030).
- M* (0.358), B0, B1, B4, B6, B9 and RETUNED (0.216) are invalid on the joint loss.
- M*J is less infeasible than B3: −0.445, one-sided upper bound −0.421 at level 0.95.
- M*Jx: also met.

**T3: Form B on the joint loss is MET.**
- M*J is valid: V2 0.049, lower bound 0.0388, upper bound 0.059; SD1_7 0.008; PD 0.004.
- Comparators valid on the joint loss: B0 (0.026) and B3 (0.021).
- M*J is less infeasible than both at level 1 − 0.05/2: −0.210 against B0 (upper bound −0.190) and −0.316 against B3 (upper bound −0.291).
- M*Jx: also met.

Per D16, (b) therefore passes its development check, so its label in the frozen plan is "pre-registered secondary", not "exploratory".

## 8. Caveats

1. **Development evidence.** λ is cross-fitted by log fold (fitted on 4 folds, applied to the 5th), but nothing here is a confirmation.
2. **Point estimates sit at the target.** M*J's V2 lies just below δ (0.048 and 0.049), as CRC intends. The one-sided upper bounds (0.058, 0.059) pass only through the +0.03 tolerance of HK1c. On Argoverse 2, a point estimate above 0.05 is plausible without any failure of the method.
3. **What the certificate covers.** It is the joint loss at the reference pose and at the full margin. The planner backs off on 42–55 % of M*J's decisions; at the executed margins the joint exceedance is 0.21–0.28. That is lower than M*'s 0.40–0.42, but it is not certified.
4. **The operability price is large.** It is +0.31 (T1) and +0.39 (T3) in infeasible rate in decision mode, which is a hard-braking indicator. M*J was not run in loop mode.
5. **M*J's base reuses its calibration scenes.** The guarantee is approximate. M*Jx is exact and gives slightly lower V2 at a slightly higher price.
6. **RETUNED is not re-tested here.** Its joint numbers come from the v03 analysis (point estimate and interval). Its CI lower bound of 0.186 is far above δ + 0.03, so it is invalid under any reading.
7. **The K7-form line printed by the v04 analysis ("GO-A") should be ignored.** It was computed on a 9-arm subset.
8. **E15 (the tie).** CRC thresholds should be decided in exact arithmetic in the AV2 analysis.
9. **A side note on process checks.** While waiting for the K8 end, one process listing displayed the command lines of another agent's processes. Nothing was opened or used, and later checks were filtered to this work root.
