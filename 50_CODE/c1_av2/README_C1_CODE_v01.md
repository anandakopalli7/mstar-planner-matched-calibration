# C1 code v01: the AV2 confirmatory pipeline (2 Nov 2026)

These files implement `40_PROTOCOL/C1_AV2_CONFIRMATORY_PREREG_v04_DRAFT.md` (where §11 and §5 disagree, §11 governs), D16 v08 Choices 6–23, D18 v02 and ERRATA_K8 E8–E15.

**Outcome-blind.** Nothing here has read an AV2 outcome. Every test and run used synthetic data, nuScenes development scenes or M*'s K7 items. The AV2 stage-B caches do not exist yet. Their schema is read from `50_CODE/av2/av2_cache_stageB_v01.py` (read only): the k6_cache_v02 layout and manifest, with one log per scene, so `k8_sim_v04` reads them unchanged.

Python: `50_CODE/env/canmot310` (3.10, NumPy 1.26.4, SciPy 1.15.3).

## The rules in `c1_analyse_v01` (C1 v04 §5.4)

- **`arm_status`: Valid and ShownAbove.** Per population: NoFlag, FamFlag, NoFlag_pool, Floor (family ≥ 6 on PD7 and SD1_7; ≥ 1 evaluable cell on PD) and HK1c. Validity is worded "not shown above δ (one-sided simultaneous test at 0.95)" (E10).
  - `floor_only` = not Valid and not ShownAbove.
  - It equals `k8_analyse.form_valid` on every planted entry and on all 30 K8 arms (reproduction).
- **`form_b`: Form B with Choice 9 (E13).** V_t is K minus the comparators shown above δ. A comparator that fails only a floor stays in, is listed in `in_V_by_choice9`, counts in |V_t| and must be beaten at 1 − 0.05/|V_t|.
- **`form_a`.** Descriptive.
- **`form_c`.** UB^{0.95}(M* − B4) ≤ c_t, with **c read from the config** (R16 m1).
- **`joint_b`: H3 on the joint loss.** ValidJ: per population the pooled joint loss is not flagged and its UB ≤ δ + 0.03. M*Jx must beat every arm of K_J = {M*, B0, B1, B3, B4, B6, B9, RETUNED} that is ValidJ. M\*J is reported (descriptive).
- **`h10_rule`.** Some PD7 cell is flagged for B6 but not for M*, with B6 − M* sup-t positive, **and** UB^{0.95}(M* − B6) ≤ 0.03.
- **`h6_rules`, `h7_rule`.** H6a = HM1, H6b = B4's HM2 failure on refit-CV, H7 = HM6, all on `m_lib_v04` summaries.
- **`h8_rule`.** X2's FAILS rule on the 4 decision cells.
- **`h9_rule`.** No flagged cell, no pooled flag, and ≥ 6 power-qualified cells with none flagged (Choice 23).
- **`holm`: family S = {H2, H3, H4, H6b, H10}.**
  - Holm at 0.05 only if H1 passes. Otherwise every claim is labelled "supportive, not confirmatory (gate H1 not passed)".
  - A claim's p-value is the maximum over trackers of its Bonferroni-adjusted rejection p-value. Validity parts are gates.
  - Family P = {H8} at 0.05.
- **Descriptive.**
  - RETUNED as E14: the fold choices, the in-sample Form-B validity of every sweep point, and the cheapest valid point.
  - Source heterogeneity: M*'s pooled V1 per population and its infeasible rate, by source, with a log-bootstrap difference.
  - The seed of every power family (R16 m5).
  - Loop mode via `k8_analyse_v04.analyse_loop`.
- **Routes and labels.**

  | Route | Label |
  |---|---|
  | `within_lt3d` | H1 primary; H2, H3, H10 secondary |
  | `transfer_lt3d` | H4 |
  | `voxelnext_val` | H5, descriptive |
  | `public_lt3d_val` | H1-P, descriptive |
  | `source_transfer` | descriptive (`c1_fit --route transfer` with folds over the held-out train logs, applied to the validation logs) |

## Full stand-in on a Linux pod (the lead's recipe)

Copy, keeping the W-relative layout: `50_CODE/c1_av2/` (all), `50_CODE/k8/k8_sim_v04.py`, `k8_run_v04.py`, `k8_analyse_v04.py`, `k8_joint_fit_v02.py`, `50_CODE/k6/*.py`, `50_CODE/m/*.py`, `50_CODE/x1/x1_lib_v03.py`, `50_CODE/k7/k7_fit_v01.py`, `30_DATA/k6/cache_v02_t{1,3}_{train,val}/`, `70_TESTS/k8_expl_v02/items_k8_v02_expl_t{1,3}.npz`. Then, from `/workspace/W/50_CODE/c1_av2`:

```
PY=/workspace/k8env/bin/python bash run_c1_standin_v01.sh 375 30 ../../70_TESTS/c1_v01/standin375 both standin 2>&1 | tee ../../70_TESTS/c1_v01/standin375_run.log
```

Expected: about 40 % of the K8 replicate's work (375 of 840 scenes, 31 arms), plus 2 fits of a few minutes each. Outputs: `70_TESTS/c1_v01/standin375/` (folds, fits, runs, `c1_analysis_v01.json`, `c1_decision_v01.json`, CSVs), every stamp "C1 STAND-IN … not evidence". Optionally, rerun the reproduction there: `c1_analyse_v01.py --config ../../70_TESTS/c1_v01/smoke60/config_reproduce_k8.json … --reproduce` (about 13 minutes on the laptop).
